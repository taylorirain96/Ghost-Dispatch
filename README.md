# Ghost-Dispatch

## The "Ghost Dispatch" Trade Multiplier

Home service businesses miss calls while on-site, which means missed revenue.  
Ghost-Dispatch acts as a lightweight AI SMS/Voice dispatch layer:

- If a customer calls and the tradesman does not answer, the AI instantly sends:
  - `"Hey, it's [Business Name] dispatch. The crew is on-site right now. What do you need quoted?"`
- The AI collects square footage or job details.
- The AI provides a rough estimate.
- The AI pushes the conversation to a qualified booked job.

## Monetization

- Zero upfront setup cost for the tradesman.
- Stripe-connected charging model.
- Strict **$15 dispatch fee** per qualified, booked job.

## Decoupled Operating Model

Ghost-Dispatch is an invisible toll bridge between homeowner demand and contractor fulfillment:

- No on-site labor.
- No manual phone answering.
- Revenue captured from intercepted missed-call opportunities.
